Watermark on the frame, and what PSNR is measured against

  codec  frame watermark      applied     PSNR reference
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  fv     2k           no            -     original
  fv     4k           no            -     original
  nv     2k           no            -     original
  nv     4k           no            -     original

  The demo build draws a watermark on the frame BEFORE encoding.
  Comparing a decoded frame with the source file would then
  measure the watermark and not the codec. So the reference is
  the frame that came back through the LOSSLESS round trip on
  the same build: it is bit for bit what the encoder was given.
  The condition: the watermark must be applied the same way
  every run; that is checked by two independent lossless round
  trips, which must come out byte for byte identical.

Energy per frame: two meters and the differential method

  dir   frame alg      codec   counter  sampling  minus idle   cores   spread
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  E     2k    irrev    fv        0.125     0.125       0.119     6.8     -0 %
  E     2k    irrev    nv        0.571     0.584       0.542    15.2     +2 %
  D     2k    irrev    fv        0.182     0.184       0.172     7.5     +1 %
  D     2k    irrev    nv        0.228     0.227       0.220     7.4     -0 %
  E     2k    rev      fv        0.227     0.228       0.219     7.2     +0 %
  E     2k    rev      nv        1.396     1.417       1.352    15.3     +2 %
  D     2k    rev      fv        0.430     0.438       0.411     7.7     +2 %
  D     2k    rev      nv        0.787     0.790       0.765     7.7     +0 %
  E     4k    irrev    fv        0.381     0.378       0.359     7.4     -1 %
  E     4k    irrev    nv        1.205     1.246       1.172    14.7     +3 %
  D     4k    irrev    fv        0.520     0.540       0.507    14.2     +4 %
  D     4k    irrev    nv        0.623     0.623       0.603     7.6     +0 %
  E     4k    rev      fv        0.738     0.743       0.712     7.5     +1 %
  E     4k    rev      nv        4.189     4.392       4.203    14.9     +5 %
  D     4k    rev      fv        1.370     1.412       1.324    14.5     +3 %
  D     4k    rev      nv        2.434     2.475       2.396     7.6     +2 %

  Two meters at once. 'counter' is the card's own cumulative
  energy counter, read through NVML, and the value is the
  DIFFERENCE between a run on 2N frames and a run on N frames,
  divided by N: everything that does not depend on the number of
  frames - process start, buffers, the card's idle draw - falls
  out of the difference. 'sampling' is the older method: average
  power over the whole run times its wall time, divided by the
  frames, so it still carries that fixed cost; 'minus idle' has
  the card's idle draw taken off it.

  The two meters differ by 1 % on the median point and by
  5 % at most. The counter is the one to quote: it loses
  nothing between samples and the difference removes the
  fixed cost of a run.
