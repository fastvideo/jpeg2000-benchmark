For both licences, attribution means naming the source and linking to it:

    Fastvideo JPEG2000 benchmark, <article URL>, run of <date>.

original page.

**Keep the measurement conditions next to the numbers** - the card, the driver,
the library versions, the file size and the measurement mode. A figure without
them is not reproducible, and a figure that outlived its conditions is worse
than no figure at all.
